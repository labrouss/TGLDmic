TGLDmic – Tiger Lake Microphone Driver

Installs the Core Audio plug-in that turns the internal digital microphones of an Intel Tiger Lake laptop into “Internal Microphone (TGLDmic)”.

Requires TGLDmic.kext loaded through OpenCore (Kernel > Add, after AppleALC) and the boot argument -tgldmic. The kext is in the separate TGLDmic-<version>-kext.zip.

The plug-in is installed to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL and Core Audio is restarted; no reboot is needed.

Unofficial community driver. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Intel, Apple or any laptop vendor.
